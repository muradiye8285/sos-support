About SoS

The goal is simple: place S and O letters to complete as many SOS lines as possible. Complete a line and you keep the turn.

Solo or 2 Players

Play against the bot or share one device for a local two-player match.

Board Sizes

Choose 5x5, 6x6, or 8x8 boards depending on how quick or strategic you want the match to feel.

Advertising and Privacy

Version 1.2 includes banner ads and occasional ads between completed matches. Advertising preferences are available when required.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026. Applies to version 1.2. This policy explains what information SoS handles and how the app respects your privacy.

Data Collection

Core gameplay runs on your device and does not require an account. Version 1.2 uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. These services may collect device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, advertising and interaction data, crash reports, performance data, and other diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics, consent management and service operation.

Ads

Version 1.2 displays Google AdMob ads. Where required, Google presents a consent form before eligible ads load. On iOS, the app requests permission before accessing the advertising identifier for tracking. If you decline tracking, the game remains available and requests non-personalized ads only when Google consent settings allow advertising. Tracking permission and advertising consent are separate choices.

Accounts

The app does not require registration, login, or a user account.

Offline Play

Core gameplay works locally on your device. The app does not need a server account to play.

Children

SoS does not ask players to enter their name, email address, or age. Apple may restrict tracking requests for child accounts. Parents can contact us with privacy questions; advertising data processing is also subject to Google’s policies.

Your Choices

Use “Ad privacy choices” in the app when shown to review Google consent choices. Manage iOS tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or in the app’s system settings. A denial does not block gameplay. Advertising requires an internet connection; core gameplay works offline.

Third-Party Services and Contact

Google processes advertising data under its Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from partner apps for details. We do not operate a player account database. For privacy questions or requests, contact gokce8535@gmail.com. Google’s retention and privacy controls apply to data processed by its services; this policy does not promise a separate deletion period for that data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
